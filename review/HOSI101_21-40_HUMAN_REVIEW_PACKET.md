## Purpose

This packet converts HOSI's abstract quality gates into a review process a real reviewer can execute. A checked box must correspond to an actual human review action. Do not pre-check boxes from AI output.

## Reviewer roles

A single person may fill more than one role only when qualified, but each role must be identified by name, relevant expertise, date, and conflict-of-interest disclosure.

### 1. Subject-matter reviewer
Checks cognitive science, learning science, memory, neuroscience, and research-literacy accuracy.

### 2. Clinical/safety reviewer
Checks clinical applications, scope boundaries, escalation language, medication/treatment boundaries, and risks of diagnostic overreach.

### 3. Lived-experience reviewer
Checks dignity, stigma, coercion, disclosure pressure, and whether examples respect heterogeneity without treating lived experience as population-level proof.

### 4. Accessibility reviewer
Checks readability, cognitive load, alt text, navigation, accessible assessment design, trauma-sensitive participation options, and disability access.

### 5. Assessment reviewer
Checks quizzes, homework, answer keys, rubrics, grading fairness, and alignment with learning objectives.

## Module-level gates

### Evidence and scholarship
- [ ] Every substantive scientific/clinical claim has a source or is clearly labeled as hypothesis, interpretation, lived experience, or founder philosophy.
- [ ] Claim wording matches the population, design, comparator, outcome, and limitations of the source.
- [ ] Meaningful contradictory or superseding evidence was searched for and recorded.
- [ ] Correlation is not presented as causation.
- [ ] Mechanistic findings are not presented as direct evidence of clinical efficacy.
- [ ] Group-level neuroscience is not used to diagnose or explain one learner.
- [ ] Older foundational sources are supplemented where newer systematic evidence materially changes interpretation.

### Safety and scope
- [ ] No lesson diagnoses a learner.
- [ ] No lesson prescribes treatment or medication changes.
- [ ] No lesson implies a diagnosis determines identity, worth, intelligence, or destiny.
- [ ] Trauma-related content allows opt-out and does not require personal disclosure.
- [ ] No suggestive memory-recovery exercises appear.
- [ ] Brain-injury material does not provide individualized prognosis or return-to-activity clearance.
- [ ] Urgent neurological or safety concerns are appropriately deferred to qualified/emergency care.

### Person-first and systems-first standards
- [ ] Ordinary human-system baselines precede diagnostic application.
- [ ] Condition-specific sections preserve heterogeneity.
- [ ] Combination/comorbidity panels do not imply equal evidence across combinations.
- [ ] Lived-experience sections are clearly distinct from universal scientific claims.
- [ ] Exercises avoid moralizing performance, motivation, memory, or behavior.

### Accessibility
- [ ] Each visual brief has useful alt text.
- [ ] Headings and lesson structure are predictable.
- [ ] Instructions are understandable without unnecessary jargon.
- [ ] Exercises provide non-disclosure alternatives where sensitive material could arise.
- [ ] Assessment design does not unnecessarily penalize disability-related speed, working-memory load, or disclosure choices.

### Assessment quality
- [ ] Knowledge checks test stated objectives.
- [ ] Homework is educational rather than diagnostic.
- [ ] Instructor answers reward evidence calibration and uncertainty.
- [ ] Rubrics grade reasoning/accuracy, not personal beliefs or treatment choices.
- [ ] No assessment implies a course score is a clinical test.

## Allowed lesson decisions

Use one of these decisions after review:

- **APPROVE FOR NEXT GATE** — reviewer found no blocking issue in their review domain.
- **APPROVE WITH NON-BLOCKING CORRECTIONS** — corrections are needed but do not invalidate the lesson's core claims.
- **REVISE AND RE-REVIEW** — one or more material claims, exercises, safety statements, or assessments require correction before advancing.
- **HOLD** — evidence is too weak, conflicting, unsafe, or incomplete to advance the lesson.

No reviewer should use **Published** as a decision. Publication is a later release decision after all applicable gates pass.
