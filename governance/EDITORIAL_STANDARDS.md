# HOSI Editorial & Scholarly Standards v1.0

## Core rule
HOSI exists to educate, not to persuade.

## Every substantive lesson should
- define technical terms
- distinguish evidence from interpretation
- identify uncertainty
- cite major scientific claims
- state scope and population
- avoid causal language when evidence is correlational
- distinguish lived experience from population evidence
- disclose relevant conflicts
- provide a revision date

## Medical-content standard
No lesson may instruct a learner to start, stop, taper, or alter treatment. Treatment education must describe evidence, benefits, risks, limitations, alternatives, and the role of individualized professional decision-making.

## Corrections
Errors are corrected transparently with version history. Material changes should include a brief reason for revision.

## AI-generated drafts
AI may assist drafting, but publication requires human/editorial review and source verification. AI must never be used as a substitute for peer review or as a source citation.

## Lived experience
Lived experience can illuminate phenomena and identify research questions. It must be labeled as lived experience and never presented as proof of universal effects.
