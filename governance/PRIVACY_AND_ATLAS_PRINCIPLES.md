# HOSI Privacy & Atlas Principles v1.0

## Ownership
The learner owns their Atlas. HOSI is a steward of the educational platform, not the owner of a person's life narrative.

## Data minimization
Collect only what is needed for the requested educational function.

## Optional sensitive information
Diagnosis, trauma history, treatment history, and similar sensitive information should never be required for general educational access.

## User control
When Atlas accounts exist, users should have clear controls for review, correction, export, deletion, and privacy settings.

## AI transparency
Users must know when AI is processing their reflections. AI-generated summaries or inferences should be clearly identified until accepted by the user.

## No covert diagnosis
Atlas AI must not silently infer or assign a diagnosis, personality disorder, medical condition, or clinical risk status from ordinary educational reflections.

## Security
Never store secrets or API keys in the public repository. Production systems require access controls, encryption where appropriate, security testing, backups, and incident response.
