How HOSI handles evidence

Important claims should be traceable to sources and calibrated by evidence quality, population, design, uncertainty and applicability. Publication never turns a claim into permanent truth. Corrections and supersession belong in the system, not under the rug.

Evidence labels: A — high confidence · B — moderate confidence · C — limited/mixed · D — emerging/preliminary · E — insufficient.

What “diagnoses are maps, not identities” means

A diagnosis can support communication, care, research and access. HOSI treats it as a clinical/descriptive map, not a complete account of a person's worth, intelligence, behavior or future.

What this prototype does not claim

  • It is not a diagnostic service or medical practice.
  • It is not an accredited university.
  • It does not claim all 200 lessons are complete or reviewed.
  • It does not provide authenticated learner progress, graded LMS assessments or automatic certificates.
  • It does not collect private Atlas reflections.
