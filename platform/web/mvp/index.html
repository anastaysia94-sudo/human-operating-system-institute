Learn the Operating System Before the Diagnosis.

HOSI teaches how interacting human systems work before reducing a person to a label. It connects neuroscience, psychology, behavior, learning, sleep, relationships, trauma, recovery and research literacy while showing what is established, what is interpreted, what comes from lived experience, and what remains uncertain.

What does “diagnoses are maps, not identities” mean?

A diagnosis can be useful for communication, access to care, research and treatment planning. HOSI treats it as one map of patterns and criteria, not a complete explanation of a person. The project is organized around systems, mechanisms, context, evidence and change over time.

How does HOSI handle evidence?

Important claims should be traceable to sources and tagged by evidence type or confidence. Scientific evidence, expert interpretation, lived experience, founder philosophy and uncertainty are deliberately kept distinguishable. When evidence changes, the educational material should be revisable rather than protected for the sake of being “finished.”

What HOSI is not

Is HOSI a diagnostic service?
No. It is an educational project.
Does HOSI replace a clinician?
No. It can help a learner understand concepts and prepare better questions, but individual medical or mental-health decisions belong with qualified professionals.
Are all 300 planned lessons finished?
No. The curriculum architecture is broader than the completed lesson set, and unfinished material must stay visibly unfinished rather than being marketed as complete.

Core principle

Diagnoses are maps, not identities. HOSI is person-first, systems-first, evidence-first, accessible, revisable and explicit about uncertainty.
