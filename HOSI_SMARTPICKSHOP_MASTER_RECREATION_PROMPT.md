==================================================
4. DIAGNOSIS AND IDENTITY
==================================================

Teach that a diagnosis is a descriptive/clinical map, not a total description of a person.

Never imply:
- a diagnosis defines worth
- a diagnosis defines intelligence
- a diagnosis predicts destiny
- a diagnosis explains every behavior
- a person is identical to a diagnostic label

Use precise, respectful language and recognize that people may have different language preferences.

==================================================
5. EVIDENCE STANDARD
==================================================

Every substantive scientific or clinical claim must distinguish:
- high-confidence evidence
- moderate-confidence evidence
- limited / mixed evidence
- emerging/preliminary evidence
- insufficient evidence
- hypothesis
- expert interpretation
- lived experience
- founder philosophy

Never:
- fabricate citations
- invent study results
- call anecdotes universal proof
- confuse correlation with causation
- infer clinical efficacy from mechanism alone
- present AI-generated references as verified

Prefer:
- primary studies
- systematic reviews
- meta-analyses
- major guidelines
- authoritative government/academic sources

For every research record, track:
- claim
- source
- source type
- year
- population
- design
- comparator
- outcome
- limitations
- contradictory evidence
- applicability
- evidence rating
- reviewer
- review date
- DOI/URL when verified

==================================================
6. PHARMACOLOGY
==================================================

The founder wants serious investigation of behavioral, cognitive, interpersonal, environmental, educational, rehabilitative, and psychological approaches rather than assuming pharmacology is the only meaningful treatment ingredient.

Preserve that mission.

DO NOT turn HOSI into blanket anti-medication propaganda.

Teach pharmacology with:
- benefits
- efficacy
- risks
- adverse effects
- interactions
- monitoring
- discontinuation considerations
- long-term evidence
- uncertainty
- individual variability
- comparative effectiveness
- patient preferences
- shared decision-making

Never advise learners to start, stop, or alter medication based on HOSI coursework.

Founder medication experiences belong in the Founder's Story as lived experience and must not automatically be generalized to all people.
