# HOSI 1.0 — Academic Catalog

HOSI uses a systems-first sequence: Human Systems → Conditions → Interventions → Integration → Scholarship.

## Study paths
Learners may study Bipolar I, Bipolar II, ADHD presentations, OCD, PTSD/trauma, brain injury/TBI, or evidence-supported combinations. The platform should adapt examples and optional panels to the learner's chosen path without treating the diagnosis as identity.

## Assessment
Formative quizzes, retrieval practice, reflective writing, case analysis, evidence appraisal, systems maps, research summaries, and a capstone.

## Credential language
Until appropriate external accreditation/authorization exists, HOSI certificates are certificates of completion and must not be represented as accredited degrees, professional licenses, or clinical qualifications.
