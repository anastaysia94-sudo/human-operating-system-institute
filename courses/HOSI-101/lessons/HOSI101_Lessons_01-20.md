# HOSI-101 — Lessons 01–20

**Development status:** substantive manuscript package exists locally; this GitHub document records the lesson-level teaching map and release controls. Full DOCX/ZIP production artifacts remain in the companion curriculum packet until binary publishing is configured.

## Module 1 — Orientation

1. **Welcome to HOSI** — purpose, scope, person-first/systems-first/evidence-first standards, education vs. clinical care.
2. **The Institute Philosophy** — diagnosis and identity, intellectual courage, uncertainty, advocacy vs. scholarship.
3. **Diagnoses Are Maps, Not Identities** — diagnostic constructs, stigma, diagnostic reductionism, personhood.
4. **What Is the Human Operating System?** — systems metaphor, interacting cognitive/emotional/behavioral/social processes.
5. **Systems Thinking** — components, interactions, feedback loops, context, hypotheses vs. evidence.
6. **How Learning Changes the Brain** — neuroplasticity, practice, feedback, constraints, adaptive vs. maladaptive change.
7. **The Scientific Method** — observation, hypotheses, study design, analysis, replication, revision.
8. **Understanding Evidence** — study types, risk of bias, directness, precision, consistency, evidence calibration.
9. **The Scholar's Oath** — humility, dignity, self-questioning, knowledge as stewardship.
10. **Module Review** — retrieval practice and systems-before-labels synthesis.

## Module 2 — Neuroscience Foundations

11. **Brain Organization** — major divisions, cortex, deep structures, networks, localization limits.
12. **The Cerebral Cortex** — lobes, specialization, distributed function, network integration.
13. **The Limbic System** — historical concept, amygdala, hippocampal formation, hypothalamus, cingulate, modern network framing.
14. **The Cerebellum** — motor coordination, prediction/error correction, emerging cognitive/affective research.
15. **The Brainstem** — midbrain, pons, medulla, arousal, autonomic/vital functions, neurological-emergency scope.
16. **Neural Networks** — structural, functional, and effective connectivity; group findings vs. individual diagnosis.
17. **Neuroplasticity** — adaptation, compensation, rehabilitation, limits of “rewiring” claims.
18. **Brain Development** — lifespan development, adolescence, individual variability, developmental assessment.
19. **Brain Health Across the Lifespan** — sleep, activity, learning, injury prevention, social factors, multidimensional health.
20. **Module Review** — integrated neuroscience map and “what this map does NOT prove” exercise.
