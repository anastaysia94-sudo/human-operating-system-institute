## Shared draft architecture

Every individual manuscript is designed to include:

- publication/review metadata;
- original visual brief and accessible alt text;
- why the topic matters;
- learning objectives and vocabulary;
- normal human-system baseline;
- core science/method;
- evidence map;
- known vs uncertain claims;
- condition-specific and combination/comorbidity lenses;
- lived-experience perspective;
- practical exercise;
- explicit safety/scope boundary;
- Cornell Notes;
- reflection and discussion;
- quiz/knowledge check;
- homework and Scholar challenge;
- instructor answer guidance/rubric;
- references and further reading;
- research update log;
- “What would change our mind?” revision trigger.
