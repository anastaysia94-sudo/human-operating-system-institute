# HOSI-101 — Understanding the Human Operating System

**200 lessons · 20 modules · capstone**

This course is the common foundation for HOSI's specialized study paths. It begins with ordinary human systems before applying them to Bipolar I/II, ADHD presentations, OCD, PTSD/trauma, and brain injury.

## Lesson architecture

Each lesson is designed to contain: original visual brief and accessible alt text; learning objectives; vocabulary; human-system baseline; core science; historical context; evidence map; what is known; uncertainty; condition-specific applications; combination/comorbidity panel; lived-experience perspective; practical skill; safety/scope; Cornell Notes; reflection; discussion prompt; knowledge check/quiz; homework; instructor answer key/rubric; references; further reading; research update log; and a “What would change our mind?” section.

Scientific and clinical claims use HOSI's evidence labels (A–E) and remain drafts until applicable human review and citation gates pass.

## Required release gates

Before any HOSI-101 lesson is labeled Published or used for final certification materials, it must pass the applicable gates:

- [ ] Full-text/current-source verification
- [ ] Evidence-strength and contradictory-evidence review
- [ ] Subject-matter review
- [ ] Medical/safety review where applicable
- [ ] Lived-experience review where applicable
- [ ] Accessibility review
- [ ] Student/instructor assessment review
- [ ] Citation verification
- [ ] Version/revision metadata
- [ ] Human approval and merge/release decision

HOSI-101 is educational and is not a medical practice, diagnostic service, treatment plan, or accredited degree program.
