# HOSI-000 — Founder's Human Operating System Atlas

## Purpose
A deep autobiographical, oral-history, reflective, and educational archive. It is **not** a clinical psychological evaluation and must not be represented as one.

1. Origins
2. Childhood
3. Family
4. Education
5. The Mind

7. Emotion
8. Health Journey
9. Adversity
10. Treatment Experiences
11. Philosophy
12. Faith
13. Intellectual Influences
14. Leadership
15. Birth of HOSI
16. Legacy

## Question object
Each question should include:
- unique ID
- prompt
- purpose
- reflection guidance
- primary narrative
- Cornell cue/notes/summary fields
- timeline/context
- memory-confidence rating
- optional corroborating evidence
- alternative interpretations
- counter-perspectives
- lessons learned
- future reflection
- related human-system tags
- related study-path tags

## Ethical constraints
- Do not diagnose the founder from the Atlas.
- Distinguish memory from corroborated fact.
- Distinguish lived experience from scientific evidence.
- Allow the founder to correct, export, and delete material where platform rules permit.
- Avoid coercive questions and unnecessary collection of sensitive information.

## Student extension
The same architecture may power learner-controlled Student Atlases, but student data must remain private by default and must never silently become institutional research data.
