# HUMAN OPERATING SYSTEM INSTITUTE (HOSI)
## Constitution — Draft 1.0

**Status:** Foundational draft. Not legal incorporation, accreditation, or medical-practice authorization.

### Preamble
HOSI is an educational project devoted to understanding the human mind, brain, behavior, relationships, learning, health, adversity, recovery, and psychological/neurological differences.

> **Learn the operating system before the diagnosis.**

Diagnoses can be useful maps of patterns, but no diagnosis is a person's identity, worth, intelligence, potential, character, or destiny. HOSI distinguishes scientific findings, hypotheses, expert opinion, lived experience, and institutional philosophy.

### Article I — Purpose
HOSI exists to teach human systems; provide structured education on bipolar I/II, ADHD, OCD, PTSD/trauma and brain injury; teach evidence-based cognitive, behavioral, interpersonal, educational and self-management approaches; build research literacy; preserve lived experience; reduce stigma; and disseminate knowledge responsibly.

HOSI is educational and does not substitute for diagnosis, emergency care, psychotherapy, medical treatment, or rehabilitation.

### Article II — Human-First Principle
All foundational teaching begins with ordinary human systems, then examines how conditions, injuries, development, environment, and context may influence them. Labels never replace the person.

### Article III — Evidence and Intellectual Honesty
HOSI shall distinguish high-confidence findings, moderate evidence, limited/mixed evidence, emerging evidence, insufficient evidence, hypotheses, expert interpretation, lived experience, and founder philosophy. No fabricated sources; no anecdote presented as universal proof; no hidden contradictory evidence; no correlation-as-causation.

### Article IV — Pharmacology and Treatment
HOSI may critically examine pharmacological interventions, including benefits, risks, adverse effects, monitoring, interactions, discontinuation considerations, individual variability, uncertainty, and long-term evidence. Non-pharmacological approaches receive the same demand for evidence. HOSI shall not declare any category universally necessary, unnecessary, harmful, or superior. Learners are taught informed and shared decision-making and appropriate professional care.

### Article V — Academic Freedom
Good-faith questioning of institutional assumptions, diagnostic frameworks, treatment paradigms, research, and founder beliefs is protected. Academic freedom does not require publication of misinformation, harassment, dangerous instructions, or discriminatory material.

### Article VI — Scholar's Oath
Graduates may pledge to avoid confusing diagnosis with identity, to recognize uncertainty, pursue understanding before judgment, question their own assumptions, preserve dignity, and use knowledge for service rather than superiority.

### Article VII — Stewardship and Faith
HOSI's founding philosophy includes: **Know thy operating system, that you may better honor your parents, faithfully steward the life entrusted to you by your Creator, love your neighbor with wisdom and compassion, and continually grow toward the person you were created to become.** This is a founding faith statement, not a scientific claim or participation requirement.

### Article VIII — Atlas Principle
The personal Atlas belongs to its author. HOSI is its steward, not its owner. Learners should be able to review, correct, export, and delete their material subject to platform/legal constraints.

### Article IX — AI Governance
AI may organize, summarize, suggest educational resources, and generate reflective questions. It must not silently diagnose, impersonate clinicians, invent sources, make treatment decisions, or claim certainty beyond evidence. AI-generated content should be identified and reviewed before becoming institutional doctrine.

### Article X — Privacy and Accessibility
HOSI should minimize personal data collection and design for mobile-first, keyboard, screen-reader, captions/transcripts, readable typography, alt text, low-bandwidth access, and accessible assessments.

### Article XI — Curriculum and Certificates
Courses require objectives, sources, evidence status, limitations, assessments, owners/editors, and revision dates. Until external accreditation/authorization exists, certificates are certificates of completion and shall not be represented as licenses, accredited degrees, or clinical qualifications.

### Article XII — Conflicts and Corrections
Relevant conflicts of interest must be disclosed. Material errors should be corrected transparently with revision history.

### Article XIII — Lived Experience
Lived experience is valuable knowledge about an individual's experience and may generate hypotheses, illuminate overlooked harms, and improve empathy; it must not be mislabeled as population-level scientific proof.

### Article XIV — Founder and Succession
The founder's story is institutional history, not infallible doctrine. Future scholars may disagree with the founder. HOSI should be designed to survive its founder.

### Article XV — Amendment
The constitution may evolve as science, ethics, technology, and governance evolve; material changes retain version history.

### North Star
> **Know thy operating system. Steward thy mind. Honor thy parents. Love thy Creator. Serve thy neighbor. Never cease learning.**
