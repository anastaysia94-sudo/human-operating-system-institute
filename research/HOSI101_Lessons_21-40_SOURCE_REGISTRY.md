## Publication gate

Before any lesson is marked Published:

1. Confirm every bibliographic field against the publisher/PubMed/official authority.
2. Read the relevant full text or authoritative guidance, not only an abstract/snippet.
3. Record population, design, comparator, outcomes, limitations, and applicability for each substantive clinical/scientific claim.
4. Search for newer systematic reviews/guidelines and meaningful contradictory evidence.
5. Have a qualified human reviewer approve the claim-to-source match.
6. Preserve corrections and the review date in the lesson research-update log.

**No source in this registry constitutes a diagnosis, treatment recommendation, accreditation claim, or proof about an individual learner.**
