# HOSI Evidence Card Schema v1.0

Each major scientific claim should be represented by an evidence card.

## Required fields
- Claim ID
- Claim
- Condition / population
- Human-system domain
- Evidence type
- Source(s)
- Publication year
- Evidence strength: A / B / C / D / E
- Risk of bias notes
- Directness / applicability
- Precision
- Consistency / heterogeneity
- Conflicts of interest, when reported
- What the evidence does NOT establish
- Date reviewed
- Reviewer/editor
- Revision history

## Evidence labels
- **A — High confidence:** consistent, relevant high-quality evidence.
- **B — Moderate confidence:** useful evidence with meaningful limitations.
- **C — Limited/mixed:** sparse or conflicting evidence.
- **D — Emerging:** early/preliminary evidence.
- **E — Insufficient:** not enough reliable evidence for a confident conclusion.

These labels are HOSI's internal educational taxonomy and are not a regulatory grading system.

## Source hierarchy
Prefer, where appropriate:
1. Major clinical guidelines and consensus documents
2. Systematic reviews/meta-analyses with appropriate methods
3. High-quality randomized trials
4. Prospective/observational research
5. Mechanistic/laboratory evidence
6. Case reports/series
7. Lived experience and testimony

No source is treated as infallible. The hierarchy informs appraisal; it does not replace appraisal.
